YouTube playback

This waits for YouTube's Skip button. It does not block ads or skip unskippable ads.

When enabled, Chrome asks for access to SponsorBlock. Segment lookups send a short hash prefix of the video ID to its service. Your IP address is visible to the service. No lookups are made while this setting is off.

Use Undo after a skip to replay it. Segment data by SponsorBlock contributors, licensed under CC BY-NC-SA 4.0.
